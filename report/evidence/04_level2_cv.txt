# 04 – Level 2: TimeSeriesSplit cross-validation

Guideline section 8. Are the Level 1 results stable, or specific to November? The models keep their **default** hyperparameters and feature set D; only the evaluation changes: five expanding-window folds on January–November, each validating 30 days (`make_tscv()`). The baselines run through exactly the same folds. Outputs: **T05** and **F12**.

**Observations.** Fold 0 (4 July–2 August) is by far the hardest for tree models and SVR: RF 5,397, XGB 6,230, SVR 8,775, DT 6,111 kW, against LR 3,071 and Naive-24h 4,024. The cause is **extrapolation**: 12% of the fold-0 hours exceed the highest load seen in training (133,130 vs 110,711 kW), and tree models (and an RBF-kernel SVR) cannot predict outside the range seen in training. From fold 2 on, RF and XGB beat LR. This motivates the **relative target** tested in notebook 05.

## 6. Summary

- With default hyperparameters **LR is the most stable** model; non-linear models are better in ordinary months but fail in July.
- The single-month ranking differs from the CV ranking, so model selection must use CV.
